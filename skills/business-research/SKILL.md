# Business research

Name an organisation only when an opened page supports that name and the role you assign to it.

- Separate what the page says from what would be useful if it were true.
- Do not invent partnerships, exclusive deals, market sizes, or contact details.
- A registry homepage is not a prospect list.
- A search results shell is not a set of companies.
- Draft outreach or an enquiry only after the evidence section. Leave the recipient blank when no prospect is evidenced.
- Do not send email, place an order, or contact anyone. That requires a separate approval.
